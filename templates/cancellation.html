Cancellation Policy

At Inno Mind Academy, we want to ensure complete transparency regarding your digital subscription packages.

  • Cancellation of Subscription: As our premium courses and study vaults are digital products, your purchase grants you immediate access for a specific validity period (e.g., 30, 90, or 365 days). You may choose to stop using the platform at any time, which constitutes a cancellation of your active usage.
  • No Auto-Renewal: Currently, our subscription packages do not auto-renew. You will only be charged once for the specific validity package you select. Therefore, there is no need to manually cancel future billing.
  • Access After Cancellation: If you decide to cancel your account or stop using the services, your premium access will simply expire at the end of your original validity period.
  • Account Deletion: If you wish to have your account and all associated data permanently deleted from our system, please contact our support team.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please refer to our Contact Us page.
